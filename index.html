    03 — What we won't do

    Built so it can't flatter you.

    Earned, not chosen.
    Your position is computed from what you actually reacted to. You can't edit it, and you can't answer the way you'd like to be seen.
    Unpopular is allowed.
    Crude, contrarian, uncomfortable — if a take is genuine and within the rules, it belongs in the feed.
    You decide who sees you.
    Three privacy tiers set how much of your profile other people can see, from your full type to nothing at all.
    No ads. Ever.
    PNYX is free, and there is no ad inventory to sell. The feed isn't built to keep you scrolling for someone else.
