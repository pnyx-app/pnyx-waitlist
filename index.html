How it works

  1. 1

    Scroll the feed

    Short videos and photos, each one posted by someone with an opinion.

  2. Tap
    Like or dislike
    Hold
    Love or hate
    2

    React to it

    Tap thumbs up or down. Hold the button down to love or hate something instead.

  3. 3

    Get your type

    After 50 reactions you get an animal and a set of colours based on how you react. It changes as you do.

  4. 4

    Find your people

    See how closely you match anyone else, as a percentage. Use it for friends, dates, or people to work with.

What's different

You can't fake it
Your type comes from what you actually react to, not from answers you pick. Nobody can edit it, including you.
Opinions are allowed
Posts can be unpopular or blunt. If it's honest and within the rules, it stays up.
You choose who sees you
Show other people your full profile, part of it, or nothing at all.
No ads
PNYX is free and doesn't show ads.
